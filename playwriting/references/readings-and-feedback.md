# Readings and feedback

Useful when there's no recording. Two readers and one listener are enough.

## During the read (listener only marks)
- **S** stumbled or rewrote the line  ·  **X** explaining  ·  **O** looked up or out  ·  **L** laugh  ·  **–** attention dropped

## Questions, in this order
1. What's it about?
2. What held you?
3. Where did you drift?
4. What did each person want in Scene __? (No hints.)
5. What confused you? What did you expect that never came?

Ask about the reaction, not the fix. Turn "why did you…?" into "how did it land that I did?" Treat suggestions as evidence of where the trouble is. "I didn't believe it" usually means not interested, or the rules changed.

## Turning marks into work
Clusters of **X** and **O**: no want. **S**: wrong rhythm or voice. **–** across a page: no new tactic. One person's taste: note it, don't chase it.

A bad performance and a bad script can look alike. Ask whether the line failed for every actor.
